In this survival horror map, you play a journalist looking into Reolosus Inc., a company that went bankrupt under strange circumstances. Your boss wants you to uncover the mystery hidden inside.

- **Singleplayer only**
- Set your brightness to **Moody**
- **Turn off the music** and turn up the volume
- The **resource pack** is included in the map
