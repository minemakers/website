**Makers Party** is a board game map with more than 15 minigames, a 150×150 board and lots of different tiles. Walk across the island in search of coins, stars and treasures!

The goal is to be the first to collect 30 stars. You earn stars by winning minigames.

First, pick your character: one of Mojang's famous faces, like Notch or Jeb. Then players take turns. On your turn, roll the dice and jump forward. There are four kinds of tiles:

- **Yellow tiles:** give you 5 coins.
- **Red tiles:** careful, they take 3 coins away.
- **Blue tiles:** start a minigame. Win it and you get between 1 and 3 stars, depending on your ranking.
- **Green tiles:** trigger special events!

Spend your coins on special items in the shop.
