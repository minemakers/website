**Color Drop** is a puzzle minigame. The goal is to be the last player alive.

You can break any group of two or more blocks of the same colour. Every block left at the end of a round costs you a life.

Break as many blocks as you can to earn points, then spend them in the shop to help you win.

**Good luck and have fun!**
