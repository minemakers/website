The goal of **Makers Run** is to run. That's all! But the blocks melt under your feet, and the last one alive wins.

There are five game modes:

- **Classic Mode:** just run. Your enemies (or you) fall through the holes.
- **Item Mode:** find items like cobwebs, swords and more.
- **Bunny Mode:** of course, *on aime le bunny*. Touch the purple particles to fly for a moment.
- **Zombie Mode:** love action? A zombie spawns every second. Dodge it, or get knocked far, far away.
- **Insane Mode:** for the best players. Creepers blow up the arena and lots of items appear.

You can also choose particles and hats.
