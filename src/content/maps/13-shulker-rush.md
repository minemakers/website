The goal of **Shulker Rush** is to kill the opposing team's Shulker, then eliminate the rest of their team. If your Shulker dies, you can't respawn anymore.

**Generators** give you nuggets: iron and gold in your spawn, and rubies from the two generators in the middle. Spend them in the shop on tools and weapons.

**Killing players earns you Souls, which you can spend to upgrade your skills.**

Good luck!
