Long ago, four heroes (a knight, an archer, a buffoon and a bomberman) freed their kingdom from Herobrine. The magician Minelin sealed his power away in an Aura held in diamond blocks. Now the monsters who survived those days are back for revenge, and they want to break the Aura and unleash Herobrine.

Pick a side:

- **Monsters** must destroy the 3 diamond blocks holding the Aura.
- **Defenders** must protect them for 30 minutes.

Kits include the Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper and Spider for the monsters (plus bonus Blaze, Enderman and Wither kits), and the Knight, Archer and Bomberman for the defenders.

For 2 to 20 players.
