The goal of **Crafty Monkeys** is to capture the five points. Stand on the wool to capture one.

You respawn at your last captured point.

Don't rush in like a berserker! **Be crafty as a monkey and find a way past your enemies.**

That's all! But it's not as simple as it sounds, Bert. You'll see!
