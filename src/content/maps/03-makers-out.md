**Makers Out** is a small PvP map. The goal is to push your opponents into the void.

- Platforms, items and a **double jump** to get around.
- Falling? Throw a bottle to build a small platform and jump back into the fight.
- Turn bonuses on or off with the signs at the spawn, and set the number of lives with **+** and **−**.
- 12 hats to wear, just for style.
