**Forget the Rules** gives you more than 40 illogical puzzles.

To solve them, you have to do the opposite of what you think. Forget the rules of a normal game!

A button? Don't press it! Gold ingots? Don't pick them up!

The map hates you so much it wants you to ragequit. Be patient, and don't try to cheat: if you exploit a bug, the map will get its revenge.

**Have a bad time! ^^**
