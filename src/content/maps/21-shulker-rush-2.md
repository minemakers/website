The goal of **Shulker Rush** is to kill the opposing team's Shulker. Once it's destroyed, that team can no longer respawn and can be wiped out.

**Iron** and **gold nuggets** appear regularly in your spawn, and **ruby generators** sit in the middle of the map. Spend all three in the shop on tools and weapons.

Killing players briefly **highlights the enemy Shulker** and gives you **Souls**, which you can spend to upgrade your skills.

Good luck!
