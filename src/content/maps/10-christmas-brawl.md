In **Christmas Brawl**, you play as a Christmas character and have to take out all your friends to be the last survivor. Catch a poisonous sock, set a bear trap or grab a chainsaw!

Look out for power-ups like the Balloon, the Musical Box and the Tinsel. Grab the **MineMakers Ball** to unleash your character's Ultimate.

- **Santa:** dazzles enemies with presents or knocks them out with a big hammer. His Ultimate takes him up in his sleigh to rain presents on his opponents.
- **Penguin:** throws stinky fish and heals with his clownfish. His Ultimate summons an army.
- **Reindeer:** his antlers are enough against beginners. With the Ultimate, even the strongest can't resist his devastating smash.
- **Snowman:** throws snowballs and explosive pumpkins. His Ultimate freezes time.
- **Elf:** strikes when you least expect it with his spanner and nail gun. His Ultimate swaps the handyman's tools for a chainsaw.
- **Boogeyman:** a formidable fighter with two swords. His Ultimate curses a player and reduces them to ashes.
- **Teddy Bear:** a symbol of peace with his axe and magic pillow... and of war. His Ultimate turns him to the dark side of the forest.

There are 5 arenas: Icy Island, Magic Jungle, Snow Village, Cloud City and Nightmare.

Designed for 2 to 12 players.
