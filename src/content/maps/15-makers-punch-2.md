In **Makers Punch**, you use knockback weapons to punch your opponents out of the arena. Jump everywhere, shoot arrows and be the last one standing.

There are two weapons:

- **Magic Stick:** for hand-to-hand combat.
- **Magic Bow:** watch out, these arrows are powerful!

Two game modes make your games even more epic:

- **Bunny Mode:** run faster and jump higher.
- **Item Mode:** lots of items like rockets, epic weapons and even a SMASHER!

There are 3 arenas, each with its own shape and quirks.
