The goal of **Piston Up** is to score the most points.

There are two roles:

- **Fisherman:** hook the jumpers and drop them into the pit to score points.
- **Jumper:** reach the end of the parkour while dodging the fisherman's hook. Use power-ups to throw the fisherman off or get a bonus.
