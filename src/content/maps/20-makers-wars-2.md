Who doesn't like Skywars? Here's the MineMakers version: choose your equipment, pick your team and have fun.

In **Makers Wars**, you have to eliminate the other teams. Push them into the void, burn them with lava, spawn mobs to do the job for you, or just use your sword.

Build your own equipment by spending points (shown on TAB). With more than 100 items, you choose your play style, from rushing the middle for the treasures to settling in for long fights.

You can also change the points limit, from 0 to 10,000, to spice up your games.
