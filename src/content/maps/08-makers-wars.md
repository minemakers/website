In **Makers Wars**, you have to eliminate the other teams. Push them into the void, burn them with lava, spawn mobs to do the job for you, or just use your sword.

Build your own equipment by spending Power points (shown on TAB). With more than 90 items, you choose your play style, from rushing the middle for the treasures to settling in for long fights, and of course trolling.

You can also change the Power limit, from 0 to 1,000, to make your games even more fun.

Can't decide? Pick one of the 8 kits:

- **Fighter Kit:** everything you need to become a good warrior.
- **Engineer Kit:** for redstone lovers. Dispensers, pistons and TNT, all you need for a TNT cannon or some tricky traps.
- **Tamer Kit:** for those who aren't afraid of monsters. What better bodyguard than a creeper?
- **Survivor Kit:** a nice collection of natural items.
- **Rusher Kit:** for air lovers who live fast.
- **Builder Kit:** for our builder friends who want to show off a fortified base.
- **Trolleur Kit:** for explosive or sticky games, of course.
- **Spy Kit:** for players who prefer to stay in the dark and strike by surprise.
