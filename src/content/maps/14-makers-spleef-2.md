The goal of **Makers Spleef** is to make your opponents fall onto the wool by mining the blocks under their feet.

There are eight game modes:

- **Classic Mode:** a pickaxe and your friends. Mine under them!
- **Item Mode:** find bonuses like blocks, potions and weird things... you'll see!
- **Arrow Mode:** no pickaxe, but a bow. Arrows blow big holes around them.
- **Bunny Mode:** more speed and higher jumps.
- **Event Mode:** random events like Swap or Acid Rain. Keep an eye on the arena!
- **Zombie Mode:** zombies fall from the sky. Run, but take out your opponents too... and don't forget to run.
- **Creeper Mode:** creepers spawn and blow up the arena around you.
- **Insane Mode:** dodge players and the explosive creepers coming from the middle, while bonuses spawn everywhere and you run faster.

There are 4 arenas and an advancements system. Unlock **new pickaxes** by pulling off great moves, like killing a friend while invisible or being resurrected twice in a row.

You can also pick particles and hats at the spawn.
